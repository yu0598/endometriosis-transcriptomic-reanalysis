# Data rights and source attribution

The MIT grant in LICENSE covers original project code and original documentation. It does not transfer rights in third-party GEO deposits, platform annotations, biological identifier databases or cited publications. Reusers must consult the linked source records and their terms. Inclusion here makes no broader licensing claim for those materials.

Processed expression matrices and sample annotations derive from GEO GSE51981 and GSE120103. Measurement-identity tables describe the specified GSE7305/GSE7307 records. Platform mapping derives from GPL570/GPL6480 and the frozen org.Hs.eg.db 3.23.1 export. All original accession identifiers and source citations are retained. See DATA_AVAILABILITY.md, REFERENCES.md, tables/S23_References50.csv and optional_raw/source_manifests/.

The fixed families, analysis outputs, audit ledgers and figure source tables were generated for this study. Source-attributed factual fields in those tables retain the provenance above. No journal full texts or private clinical records are distributed.
